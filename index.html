About OrbieRobot

A Robot Built for Every Pet

OrbieRobot is an open-source robotics platform designed from the ground up to interact with, monitor, and enrich the lives of dogs, cats, and other companion animals.

Open Source

Every line of code, every schematic is freely available. Fork it, modify it, make it yours. Built on the belief the best pet tech comes from an open community.

ESP32-S3 Powered

A few-dollar chip that runs perception, audio, and motor control. Dual-core processor with WiFi and BLE for seamless pet interaction.

Camera & Audio

OV5640 camera and built-in speaker and microphone. Talk to your pet, hear them respond, and check in with live video from anywhere.

Robust Mobility

Dual N20 gear motors with DRV8833 driver for precise differential steering. Program custom patrol routes and autonomous behavior.

Expressive Face

Dot-matrix LED eyes driven by IS31FL3733. Fully programmable expressions that make every interaction feel personal and alive.

DIY Friendly

Designed for makers of all skill levels. 3D-printable chassis, detailed build guides, and a welcoming community help you get started.

01 — Three Generations

Built Three Times in Eight Months

From a transparent ball to a companion with a face. OrbieRobot evolved through three distinct generations in under a year.

V1 Jul — Sep 2025
The Ball

A Replication

A replica of the open-source ESP-ROLL project, built to learn what would and would not work. Transparent shell, everything visible.

Large battery at the bottom doubled as ballast to make the ball roll. The motors and PCB barely fit the 3D-printed parts. Speaker worked, but the sound was too low.

01
V2 Sep 2025 — Jan 2026
Ten Units

Our Own Rolling Design

Built ten times over. Still transparent, still nothing hidden.

We received the wrong part number — DRV8834 instead of DRV8833, but made it work. First full unit assembled 14 Oct 2025. Learned what mattered and what could be cut. Firmware running on ESP-IDF from day one.

10
V3 2026 — Current
The Companion

A Complete Rethink

Two wheels, a tilting head, dot-matrix eyes that emote.

It stopped being a ball and started being someone. The LED face chosen over RGB in December 2025. Three servos for head tilting — enough movement to read as attention rather than animation. The OrbieRobot we know today.

Getting Started

From Zero to Robot in Four Steps

OrbieRobot is designed to be accessible whether you are a seasoned engineer or a curious maker.

1

Gather Components

Source the ESP32-S3 board, OV2640 camera, DRV8833 motors, chassis, and sensor modules. A complete bill of materials is available.

2

Build the Hardware

Follow the detailed assembly guides with step-by-step instructions, wiring diagrams, and 3D-printable chassis files.

3

Flash the Firmware

Clone the repository and flash the firmware using ESP-IDF. Pre-built binaries are available for quick starts.

4

Connect & Customize

Connect to the OrbieRobot WiFi access point, open the web interface, and start interacting with your pet.
